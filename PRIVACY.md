# Privacy

Product Expiration Easy Peasy stores expiration dates as product metadata, stores its settings in the WordPress options table, and stores a hidden purchase-time expiration snapshot on new WooCommerce order items.

The expiration feature itself does not add analytics, advertising, or marketing telemetry. Notification emails are delivered through the WordPress mail transport configured by the site owner.

The generated RTL-Theme license runtime may communicate with RTL-Theme to validate a purchase code, license state, and registered domain. Site owners should review the terms and privacy information supplied by RTL-Theme for that service.

On uninstall, the plugin restores stock states it previously managed and removes its settings, cron event, locks, product expiration metadata, stock-management markers, and hidden order-item snapshots.
